# Calendar

**Calendar** (sidebar, Library group) shows the movie releases and episode air dates of your library, and what is
already on disk. Movies appear on their cinema, digital and physical release dates, episodes on their air date.

- Views: **Month**, **Week** and **List**; Previous and Next move through time.
- Choose movies, series or both; *Show unmonitored* adds unmonitored items.
- A colour legend tells downloaded, downloading, missing and not released yet apart. Click a day for its list, an
  entry for its detail page.

The first day of the week is set in Settings → UI.

## iCal feed

**iCal link** gives an address that calendar apps (Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar, Thunderbird) can
subscribe to:

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Past days, Future days | How far back and ahead the feed reaches |
| Movies, Series | What the feed contains |
| Include unmonitored | Also unmonitored items |

**Copy iCal link** or **Open in calendar app**. The link carries its own read-only token: it opens only this
calendar, but anyone who has the link can read it. **Create new link** ends the old one (calendar apps with the old
link stop updating). Feed links from before 0.12, which contained the API key, no longer work; copy the new link.
